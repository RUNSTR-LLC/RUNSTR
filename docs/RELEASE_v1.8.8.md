# RUNSTR v1.8.8: Social

RUNSTR has come a long way. From a UX perspective, from a rewards perspective, from a usability perspective, and now we are turning the corner into something that more closely resembles our final form. RUNSTR is the Nostr-native fitness tracker with Bitcoin rewards, but there is a lot to unpack in that sentence. At its core, the value proposition is simple: RUNSTR rewards you for your fitness endeavors and serves as your social fitness client. The app has evolved from a basic run tracker into a full activity platform covering cardio, strength, wellness, and mindfulness, with a reward system that lets you decide where your earnings go. Whether your workouts fund a charity, support an open source project, pay for AI credits, or go straight to your wallet, the choice is yours and you can change it anytime. Version 1.8.8 represents the most complete expression of that vision to date.

The social layer is where 1.8.8 makes its biggest leap. We tackle social fitness in three ways: a feed, club chatrooms, and virtual competitions. The Social tab now features a Nostr-powered feed where you can see workout posts from the community, leave comments, send zaps, and interact with other athletes directly from within the app. Club chatrooms have been overhauled with full-screen chat, pinned messages, and push notifications so you never miss a message from your crew. Competitions have been expanded with prize pool support, charity event templates, and a simplified event creation flow that lets club captains spin up races and challenges from templates in seconds. Every workout you log, whether from RUNSTR's built-in trackers or synced automatically from your Apple Watch, Garmin, Strava, or any other app connected to Apple Health or Health Connect, counts toward leaderboards and competitions without you lifting a finger.

RUNSTR Season III is coming in May, and it will be free to join. Season III introduces Club Battles, a 16-team double-elimination tournament where Fitness Clubs compete against each other for a grand prize. Clubs are matched up, their members' workouts are scored, and the losing club drops to the losers bracket while the winner advances. It is community fitness competition at its most engaging. At the end of Season III, we will also donate to the most represented charity among tournament participants. Every user selects a reward destination, and the charity or project with the most users backing it during the competition receives a collective donation from the tournament. It is a way to turn collective fitness effort into collective impact.

Beyond the headline features, 1.8.8 is packed with quality of life improvements that make RUNSTR more private, more reliable, and more rewarding than ever. You can log in with Nostr or spin up a fresh identity by tapping Start, no email or account required. You can encrypt and back up your workout history to Nostr relays, and restore everything if you switch devices. The app pulls in workouts from across wearables and fitness apps on your phone through HealthKit and Health Connect, submitting them to leaderboards and triggering rewards in the background. You can post any workout directly to Nostr with one tap. Privacy Mode ensures all workouts stay local on your device if you prefer. We do not collect GPS points, we do not collect personal information, and the app is more privacy-preserving than most fitness trackers on the market. The streak accumulator now enhances your daily workout reward, giving you a 10 to 40 percent bonus for maintaining consecutive workout days.

At its foundation, RUNSTR is a Nostr-native fitness app with Bitcoin rewards. Nostr gives us a decentralized identity layer and social features that extend beyond the app itself, as well as a decentralized database to encrypt and store basic workout summaries. The Bitcoin rewards piece operates through micro payments sent to your wallet, your charity, your project, or sent as payment to PPQ.AI for AI credits. We also have side features like Wavlake and Blossom music integration for your workout soundtrack. Version 1.8.8 ships with 177 new features, 149 bug fixes, 19 database migrations, and thousands of lines of dead code removed. The app is cleaner, faster, and more focused than it has ever been. Find out more about the project at www.runstr.club, and join us for Season III in May.
