## What's New in v1.6.6-debug

I've removed the compete button from the post workout stuff so that workouts/steps automatically go into competitions.

I've gated some features by WOT, so if you're a regular nostr user you'll see some nostr options like posting workout summaries/wavlake toggle in settings.

I've made the walk screen passive, it shows steps from health connect and estimates distance from steps and throws that into competitions.

I've updated the rewards screen to show a rewards pool, breakdown of where the rewards went, and your earnings. If you don't have a lightning address put into the app then it shows charitable contributions rather than earnings.

I've updated Coach RUNSTR, calling it RUNSTR AI now. You can select ppq.ai as a team and create an anonymous account with one click, your rewards can be sent to top up your PPQ balance. There's a journal/habit tracker/AI chat interface that allows you to select a modal and ask questions about your health and fitness.

Added the ability to encrypt and save your workout history to nostr, and the option to choose the relay(s) you want it sent to. (So you could have it sent to citrine or relaytools relay you spun up.) Added the ability to import that workout history into your phone's local storage in RUNSTR.

Brought back the blossom servers as music playlists feature, seeing now that there is more support for that kind of thing.

Instant rewards if steps hit 10k or a run hits 3k.

Bunch of bug fixes and performance improvements.

---

### Key Features:
- Step tracker with distance estimation and posting functionality at the top of activity trackers
- Swipe to switch tracker
- Stats in history
- RUNSTR AI in history
- Rewards pool transparency
- Nostr users see Wavlake option and posting option
- Bitcoiners with lightning address see earnings and get rewards
- Normies fund charities and see charity breakdown
- 1km+ cardio earns 50 sats or 3k+ steps for the day
