# Track Naming Conventions - Night Grind Radio

## Brand Voice
Dark, minimalist, grind-culture aesthetic. Think: late-night study sessions, monk-mode focus, building in silence.

## Naming Style
- Short, evocative phrases (2-4 words)
- Dark academia / grind culture tone
- Reference: architecture, shadows, fog, night, manuscripts, silence, discipline
- Avoid: happy, bright, upbeat, party words

## Process
1. Auto-generate names from the beat filenames as starting inspiration
2. Replace with on-brand names following the style above
3. Present names to user for approval before proceeding
