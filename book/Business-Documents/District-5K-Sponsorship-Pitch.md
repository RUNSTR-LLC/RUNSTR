# RUNSTR PRESENTS

# The District 5K
**A Charity Running Competition**

---

## THE EVENT

**Location:** Haines Point, Washington DC
**Date:** March 15, 2026 | 7:00 AM - 10:00 AM

A scenic 5K race along the Potomac where every runner supports a charity. Participants choose their team—Human Rights Foundation, ALS Network, or Chimes International—and race to put their charity on top.

**How It Works:**
- Pick your charity at registration
- Wear your charity sticker during the race
- Run your best 5K
- The charity with the most runners in the **Top 100 finishers wins $500**

**Audience:** Runners, fitness enthusiasts, and walkers from the DC metro area

---

## ABOUT RUNSTR

RUNSTR is a fitness event company connecting running with charitable giving.

Our mobile app lets runners:
- Track and record runs with GPS
- Compete in virtual fitness competitions
- Earn rewards that support charity

**Our model:** Profits from in-person events fund our in-app rewards pool—so every race helps runners everywhere support causes they care about.
