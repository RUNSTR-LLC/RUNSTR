# RUNSTR Team Project Explainer

**Internal Team Summary | January 2026**

---

## What RUNSTR Is

RUNSTR is a fitness event company that operates both virtual and in-person competitions, powered by a mobile app that tracks running, walking, and cycling workouts. The app rewards users for completing workouts—real rewards, not points. We're building a company at the intersection of fitness and charitable giving, where every workout can support a cause the runner cares about. The app is privacy-focused by design: no email, phone number, or real name required.

## The Three Core Pillars

The business is built on three interconnected pillars: Workouts, Events, and Rewards. The Workouts pillar provides GPS tracking for cardio activities with real-time metrics (pace, distance, elevation, splits) plus integration with Apple Health and Google Health Connect. The Events pillar hosts both virtual competitions through the app (with leaderboards and prizes) and in-person races like our upcoming District 5K. The Rewards pillar creates a positive feedback loop where fitness literally earns money for users and charities.

## The Charity Integration

Teams in RUNSTR represent charities. When users select a "team," they're choosing a charity to support—organizations like Human Rights Foundation, ALS Network, Chimes International, and 15+ others. The District 5K takes this further: participants wear stickers showing which charity they're running for, and the charity with the most runners in the Top 100 finishers wins $500. Every race becomes a competition not just between individuals, but between causes.

## The Events Business Model

Our path to profitability centers on in-person 5K races. Each event targets ~$5,000 profit from registration fees and sponsorships. The inaugural race—The District 5K—launches March 15, 2026 at Haines Point, Washington DC. We're targeting local sponsors: running stores (Pacers, Fleet Feet), gyms and CrossFit boxes, sports recovery clinics, nutrition shops, and local coffee roasters. Two initial sponsorship tiers: $500 Community Sponsor and $1,000 Featured Sponsor. Profits from in-person events fund our in-app rewards pool.

## The Strategy

We're proving the concept in DC first, then using that data to pitch expansion to Austin (Q2), Nashville (Q3), and Mexico (Q4). For sponsorships, we're leading with local, non-tech businesses—running stores, gyms, health food shops, coffee roasters. We intentionally cap sponsor numbers to protect partner visibility. The pitch is simple: support a community event that connects fitness with charitable giving, get your brand in front of engaged runners and fitness enthusiasts, and be part of something meaningful.
