## Why Sponsor RUNSTR Events?

RUNSTR produces 5K race events that bring together two powerful audiences:
- **Fitness Enthusiasts:** Health-conscious runners seeking community and competition
- **Bitcoin/Nostr Community:** Tech-forward early adopters with high engagement

Our events feature:
- Professional race management with timing and results
- Post-race brunch and community gathering
- Live YouTube broadcast reaching thousands
- Virtual participation expanding reach globally
- Charitable giving component that resonates with participants

**Audience Mix:** 80% mainstream fitness enthusiasts, 20% Bitcoin community members

## Sponsorship Tiers

### Tier 1: Community Supporter — $500

*Perfect for local businesses wanting race day presence*

**Benefits:**
- Logo displayed on event website
- Social media mention (1 post across platforms)
- Company name in email communications to participants
- 2 complimentary race entries

**Ideal For:** Local coffee shops, nutrition stores, small fitness businesses

---

### Tier 2: Bronze Sponsor — $1,000

*Great visibility with meaningful brand exposure*

**Benefits:**
- **All Tier 1 benefits, plus:**
- Logo on race day banner at start/finish area
- Verbal mention during post-race brunch
- Logo in post-race email to all participants
- Featured in "Thank You" segment of race recap video
- 4 complimentary race entries

**Ideal For:** Running stores, sports nutrition brands, local gyms, physical therapy clinics

---

### Tier 3: Silver Sponsor — $2,500

*Premium exposure with significant brand integration*

**Benefits:**
- **All Tier 2 benefits, plus:**
- Logo on official race t-shirt
- Booth space at post-race venue (table + signage)
- Mention in YouTube live broadcast commentary
- Featured sponsor highlight video (60 seconds)
- Logo watermark on finish line photos
- Social media feature story (dedicated post)
- 8 complimentary race entries

**Ideal For:** Regional athletic brands, health food companies, sports drink brands, fitness equipment companies

---

### Tier 4: Gold Sponsor — $5,000+

*Title-level sponsorship with maximum brand impact*

**Benefits:**
- **All Silver benefits, plus:**
- Named sponsor of a race element (choose one):
  - "The [Brand] Finish Line"
  - "The [Brand] Water Station"
  - "The [Brand] Start Corral"
- Premium booth placement at post-race venue
- Dedicated sponsor highlight video (2-3 minutes)
- Logo on race bib numbers
- Speaking opportunity at post-race brunch (2 minutes)
- Featured in all promotional materials
- First right of refusal for future events
- 15 complimentary race entries
- VIP access to RUNSTR team

**Ideal For:** Major athletic brands, national sponsors, Bitcoin companies seeking mainstream exposure
