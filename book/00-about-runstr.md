# About RUNSTR

RUNSTR is a Nostr fitness client that combines the social and value layer of Primal with the workout tracking and competition features of Strava. The app aggregates workouts from any device or fitness app connected to Apple Health or Health Connect, enters them into virtual competitions, and rewards users for staying active. Whether you track a run directly in RUNSTR with full GPS telemetry or log a walk from your Apple Watch, the workout flows into the same system. RUNSTR is built around three pillars: workouts that sync from anywhere, a social layer with competitions and Fitness Clubs, and a rewards system that lets users choose how they receive what they earn.

RUNSTR supports four categories of activity across a swipeable grid. Cardio covers running, walking, cycling, and hiking with real-time GPS tracking including pace, distance, elevation, and per-kilometer splits. Strength includes pushups, pull-ups, sit-ups, squats, curls, and bench press with rep and set counting. Wellness offers guided meditation, unguided meditation, breathwork, body scan, and gratitude sessions. Mindfulness covers journaling and habit tracking. But you don't have to use RUNSTR's built-in trackers at all. The app works with any device or fitness app connected to Apple Health or Health Connect. Garmin, Fitbit, Apple Watch, Nike Run Club, Strava, whatever you already use. On iOS, HealthKit background delivery wakes the app whenever a new workout appears. On Android, a periodic sync via Health Connect runs every fifteen minutes. You can go for a run with your Garmin, finish, and by the time you check your phone, RUNSTR has already pulled the workout, submitted it to the daily leaderboard, sent your reward, and optionally posted it to the social feed. Workout history is encrypted and backed up to Nostr relays so if you switch phones or reinstall, everything restores seamlessly. For users who want full privacy, a local-only Private Mode lets you track workouts without participating in any networked features.

The social feed pulls in fitness-related posts from across the Nostr network, not just RUNSTR users, but anyone posting about workouts, races, or training. You can like, zap, repost, and comment directly from the feed, creating a fitness-first social experience. Fitness Clubs add a layer of community on top of this: club captains get a dedicated page with a member leaderboard, real-time chat, and the ability to create events for their members. Competitions are central to the experience. A daily leaderboard tracks the fastest 5K, 10K, half marathon, and full marathon times alongside a daily steps ranking, all updated in real time and always active. Beyond the daily boards, featured events run on schedules with specific goals like distance challenges, streak competitions, and team-based races. Club captains can create events from templates like a 5K charity run, a 10K challenge, or a step competition, and all club members are automatically entered. The platform is moving toward fully user-created competitions where anyone can design, host, and fund their own events with custom prize pools and charity payouts.

Every qualifying workout earns the user 50 sats, and RUNSTR lets users choose how they receive their rewards across four categories of destinations: charities like the ALS Network and Human Rights Foundation, grassroots projects like Bitcoin Beach and Bitcoin Ekasi, services like PPQ.AI where rewards convert into AI credits, or their own Lightning wallet. Pick one destination, change it anytime. A lottery wheel adds an additional reward layer, giving users a spin after qualifying workouts. Your RUNSTR level, which is a direct correlation to your workout history, determines the reward multiplier on the wheel. The more you work out, the higher your level, the better your lottery odds and payouts. The reward system is sponsor-funded through what RUNSTR calls Zapvertising. Sponsors fund the reward pool and their brand attribution appears on the Rewards page and in push notifications, keeping rewards sustainable without selling user data or charging users.

RUNSTR is building toward a future where fitness communities are self-sovereign. Club captains will connect NWC wallets to create non-custodial reward pools and prize pools for their events. Users will create their own competitions, a charity 5K with a real prize pool, a corporate wellness challenge, a neighborhood step competition. The daily leaderboard stays built-in and always running, but everything else becomes user-generated. The app already handles workout aggregation, encrypted backup, background sync, social engagement, and reward delivery. What comes next is giving that power to the community, letting anyone host events, fund prizes, and build fitness economies on open rails. RUNSTR is a fitness company that happens to run on Nostr and Lightning, and that distinction is the point.
