## Writing Prompts for Dakota

### Introduction - The Hook
1. What moment made you realize you wanted to build something different in the fitness app space?
2. If you had to describe RUNSTR in one sentence to someone who's never heard of it, what would you say?
3. What were you doing (literally, in your life) when the idea for RUNSTR crystallized?

### Body Paragraph 1 - The Nosfabrica Challenge
4. What is Nosfabrica and how did you get involved?
5. What was the challenge prompt or problem you were trying to solve?
6. How long did you have to build the initial version?
7. What was the atmosphere like during the challenge - competitive, collaborative, chaotic?
8. What did you build during that initial sprint, and what did it look like?

### Body Paragraph 2 - PlebLab Startup School
9. How did you go from the Nosfabrica challenge to PlebLab Startup School?
10. What did Startup School teach you that the hackathon couldn't?
11. Who were the mentors or people who shaped your thinking?
12. What was the hardest feedback you received, and how did it change the product?
13. What moment during Startup School made you think "this could actually work"?

### Body Paragraph 3 - The Vision
14. What problem in the fitness app world genuinely frustrates you?
15. Why do most fitness apps feel hollow or unsatisfying to you?
16. What does "fitness tracker that means something" mean to you personally?
17. How does your own fitness journey inform what you're building?
18. What do you want someone to feel when they complete a workout with RUNSTR?

### Conclusion - Looking Forward
19. What's the one thing you hope readers take away from this article?
20. If RUNSTR succeeds wildly, what does the world look like in 5 years?
21. What would you tell someone who's skeptical that another fitness app can make a difference?

## Key Themes to Weave In
- Personal authenticity (this isn't corporate, it's you)
- The gap between existing fitness apps and what people actually need
- Building in public / startup journey
- Community and collaboration in the building process

## Avoid

- Industry jargon
- Making it sound like a pitch deck
