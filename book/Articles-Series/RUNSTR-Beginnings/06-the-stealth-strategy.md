## Key Themes to Weave In
- Meet people where they are, not where you want them to be
- Technology should be invisible to end users
- Authenticity in marketing
- The "Trojan horse" of useful technology

## Concrete Examples to Include
- Actual different descriptions used for different audiences
- Platform-specific content strategies
- Before/after of messaging evolution

## Messaging Angles to Explore
- Fitness-first: "A fitness app that pays you to work out"
- Charity-first: "Turn your workouts into donations"
- Privacy-first: "The anonymous fitness tracker"
- Community-first: "Compete without giving up your privacy"

## Avoid
- Making it sound manipulative
- Revealing technical details that contradict "stealth"
- Being preachy about marketing philosophy
