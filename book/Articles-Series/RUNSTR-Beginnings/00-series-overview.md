# RUNSTR Beginnings - Article Series Overview

## About This Series
A 7-part article series telling the RUNSTR story for a general audience. Each article is designed to stand alone while building toward a complete picture of what RUNSTR offers and why it matters.

## Target Audience
- General public curious about fitness apps
- Fitness enthusiasts looking for something meaningful
- People interested in charitable giving through everyday actions
- Privacy-conscious individuals

## Tone Guidelines
- **No jargon**: Avoid Bitcoin/Nostr technical terms
- **Accessible**: Write for someone who's never heard of cryptocurrency
- **Inspiring**: Focus on human impact and personal stories
- **Conversational**: Like talking to a friend about something you're excited about

## Article Structure
Each article follows a consistent format:
1. **Introduction** - Hook the reader, establish the topic
2. **Body Paragraph 1** - Core concept or story element
3. **Body Paragraph 2** - Deeper exploration or evidence
4. **Body Paragraph 3** - Personal connection or broader implications
5. **Conclusion** - Tie it together, hint at what's next

## Series Flow

| # | Title | Core Message |
|---|-------|--------------|
| 01 | Why I Built RUNSTR | The origin story and driving vision |
| 02 | Fitness That Gives Back | Rewards that become charitable impact |
| 03 | Anonymous by Design | Privacy without sacrificing community |
| 04 | Your AI Coach Gets Smarter | Personalization that respects you |
| 05 | Powering Grassroots Good | Small actions, big collective impact |
| 06 | The Stealth Strategy | Meeting people where they are |
| 07 | Built for Everyone | Why this matters for everyday people |

## Publishing Strategy
- Each article can be published independently on blogs, Medium, LinkedIn
- Series works as a cohesive narrative when read together
- Can be adapted for podcast scripts or video content
