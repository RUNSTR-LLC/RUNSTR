## Writing Prompts for Dakota

### Introduction - The Problem
1. What's broken about the typical fitness app reward system (badges, streaks, virtual trophies)?
2. Have you ever earned a badge in an app and thought "so what?" - describe that feeling.
3. What would it take for fitness rewards to actually matter?

### Body Paragraph 1 - The Reward System
4. Without getting technical, how does earning rewards in RUNSTR work from the user's perspective?
5. What does it feel like to finish a workout and see actual value earned (not points)?
6. How do you explain "you earn real money for working out" without it sounding like a scam?
7. What's the simplest way to describe the reward amounts (daily workout, steps, etc.)?

### Body Paragraph 2 - The Donation Split
8. How does the donation split work from the user's perspective?
9. Why did you choose to let users pick their own charity rather than a single cause?
10. What happens when someone sets their donation split - where does that money actually go?
11. Can you tell a story (real or hypothetical) of how small workout donations add up over time?
12. What charities are currently available, and why those specifically?

### Body Paragraph 3 - The Emotional Impact
13. What's the difference between running for yourself vs. running for something bigger?
14. Have you received any feedback from users about how the donation feature affected their motivation?
15. How does knowing your workout helps others change the workout itself?
16. What's the most meaningful thing someone could accomplish through consistent RUNSTR use over a year?

### Conclusion - The Vision
17. If every fitness app worked this way, how would the world be different?
18. What's stopping other apps from doing this, and why can RUNSTR do it?
19. What do you want a reader to do after finishing this article?

## Key Themes to Weave In
- Intrinsic vs. extrinsic motivation
- The power of small, consistent contributions
- Making fitness feel meaningful beyond personal vanity
- Connecting individual action to collective good

## Concrete Examples to Include
- Specific reward amounts (50 sats daily, 5 sats per 1k steps) translated to real-world value
- Example charities and what they do
- Math: "If 1,000 people walked 5k steps daily, that's X going to charity per month"

## Avoid
- Making it sound too good to be true
- Getting into cryptocurrency mechanics
- Preaching about why people should donate
