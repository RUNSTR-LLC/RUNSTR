## Writing Prompts for Dakota

### Introduction - The Privacy Problem
1. What do most fitness apps know about you that feels invasive?
2. Have you ever hesitated to use a fitness feature because of privacy concerns?
3. Why should someone care about fitness app privacy - what's the real risk?
4. What's the creepiest thing you've learned about how fitness data gets used or sold?

### Body Paragraph 1 - How RUNSTR Does It Differently
5. What information does RUNSTR NOT collect that other apps do?
6. How can someone compete on leaderboards without revealing who they are?
7. What does the sign-up process look like - what do you need to provide?
8. How do you verify workouts without tracking someone's every move?
9. Can you explain the identity system without using technical terms?

### Body Paragraph 2 - Community Without Compromise
10. How do people connect with each other in RUNSTR if everyone's anonymous?
11. What does a leaderboard look like when no one uses real names?
12. How do teams work when members don't know each other's identities?
13. Is there any way to be "more public" if someone wants to be?
14. How does the social/sharing feature work with privacy in mind?

### Body Paragraph 3 - Why This Matters
15. Who is the person most helped by anonymous fitness tracking?
16. Tell a story about someone who might want to track fitness privately (abuse survivor, public figure, someone in a conservative culture, etc.)
17. What's the difference between privacy and secrecy - how do you think about that distinction?
18. How does privacy actually enable better fitness tracking, not worse?

### Conclusion - The Bigger Picture
19. What would you say to someone who thinks "I have nothing to hide"?
20. How does RUNSTR's approach to privacy reflect your personal values?
21. What do you hope becomes standard in fitness apps because of what you're building?

## Key Themes to Weave In
- Privacy as a feature, not a limitation
- The false choice between community and anonymity
- Real-world examples of why fitness privacy matters
- Dignity and autonomy in personal health tracking

## Concrete Examples to Include
- What data other apps collect vs. RUNSTR
- Specific privacy-preserving features
- Use cases for different types of users who need privacy

## Avoid
- Making it sound paranoid or fear-based
- Technical explanations of cryptography or protocols
- Implying other apps are evil (focus on what RUNSTR does right)
