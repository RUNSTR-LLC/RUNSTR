### Introduction - The AI Opportunity
1. What's wrong with most fitness app coaching - why does it feel generic?
2. What would a truly personalized fitness experience look like?
3. How do you think about AI as a coaching tool vs. AI as surveillance?

### Body Paragraph 1 - Learning Your Patterns
4. What does RUNSTR learn about you over time?
5. How does the app adapt its suggestions or interface based on usage?
6. What's an example of the app "getting smarter" for a regular user?
7. How does learning happen without compromising privacy?
8. What patterns are most useful for improving someone's fitness experience?

### Body Paragraph 2 - Progressive Feature Unlocking
9. What features are hidden or simplified for new users?
10. Why did you choose to shield certain features rather than show everything upfront?
11. How does someone "unlock" more advanced features?
12. What's the philosophy behind progressive complexity?
13. Give an example of a feature journey - what does a user see at week 1 vs. month 3?

### Body Paragraph 3 - Rewarding Engagement
14. How does deeper engagement with the app get rewarded?
15. What's the difference between rewarding engagement vs. creating addiction?
16. How do you think about the ethics of gamification in fitness?
17. What guardrails exist to prevent unhealthy app dependence?
18. How does the reward system encourage healthy habits, not just app usage?

### Conclusion - The Future
19. What AI features are you most excited to build next?
20. How do you balance personalization with simplicity?
21. What's your vision for AI coaching that other apps haven't achieved?
