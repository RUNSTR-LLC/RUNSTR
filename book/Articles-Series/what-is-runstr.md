# What is RUNSTR?

RUNSTR is a fitness app that turns everyday workouts into a rewarding social experience. At its core, the app tracks cardio activities like running, walking, cycling, and hiking using real-time GPS, while also supporting strength exercises, meditation, journaling, and habit tracking through dedicated screens. Users don't need to actively open the app to participate — HealthKit on iOS and Health Connect on Android sync workouts automatically in the background without any manual intervention. The app is designed around three tabs — Profile, Social, and Events — each representing a pillar of the experience.

The rewards system is what makes RUNSTR unique among fitness trackers. Every workout triggers a service that sends real rewards to the user's chosen destination — either their own wallet, a charity like the ALS Foundation, an open source project, or a service like PPQ.AI for AI credits. Users pick one destination and all their rewards flow there. The Rewards screen also features a daily lottery wheel spin where users can win bonus rewards, with a multiplier that scales with their level. Sponsors fund the reward pool through "Zapvertising" — branded push notifications and attribution banners that create a sustainable revenue model that doesn't charge users.

The social layer is built on the Nostr protocol and centered around Fitness Clubs and a community feed. The Social tab displays a chronological feed of fitness notes where users share workout achievements as Nostr events, complete with generated achievement cards and images. Users can zap each other directly from the feed to show support. Fitness Clubs function as team spaces where members compete together, chat in real-time, and participate in club-specific events created by their captain. Club chat supports pinned messages, workout auto-sharing, and captain-managed moderation. When a user joins a club, their workouts are automatically tagged with the club, contributing to the club's collective leaderboard.

Competitions tie the workout and social systems together. The Events tab shows a mix of always-active daily leaderboards (5K, 10K, Half Marathon, Marathon, Steps), seasonal competitions, and dynamic events created by club captains or featured by the platform. When a user completes a workout, the app automatically joins them into eligible competitions and submits their data to the appropriate leaderboards. Scoring reflects both server-confirmed data and optimistic local entries so users see their progress immediately after finishing a workout.

RUNSTR is an open source project built on Nostr as its identity and social backbone. Users can create a Nostr identity on sign-up or log in with an existing one, and the app automatically encrypts and backs up their workout history to Nostr relays for portability and data ownership. Privacy is a core principle — no personally identifiable information is required to join or earn rewards, GPS points are never stored in any database, and users can choose to work out completely privately with all data saved only on their device, never sent to Nostr or any backend. The app gives users full control over what they share and what stays on their phone.
